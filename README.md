# Material Details (For Mechanical Enclosure Design)
 **"1- Black Polycarbonate"** Properties
 High impact resistance, ensuring durability in challenging environments.
 UV resistance to prevent discoloration or degradation under sunlight.
 Lightweight yet robust, making it ideal for the main body of the enclosure.
 **Applications**
  Primary enclosure body for mechanical and environmental protection.
 **"2- Transparent Polycarbonate"** Properties
   Optical clarity with up to 90% light transmission for visibility.
 Excellent impact resistance to withstand shocks without cracking.
 UV resistance to prevent yellowing and maintain transparency over time.
 **Applications:**
 Inspection windows or areas where visibility is required for internal components.
 Both materials are carefully selected to align with IP67 standards, ensuring optimal protection and durability.
**3- UV-resistant Polycarbonate Coating**  Properties
 Best for: Transparent enclosures or any plastic component exposed to sunlight.
 Advantages: Protects the polycarbonate from UV degradation, yellowing, and surface damage, maintaining clarity and durability over time.
 **4-Silicone Rubber**  Properties
  Best for: Seals and gaskets for outdoor, industrial, and waterproof enclosures.
 Advantages: Excellent flexibility, water resistance, and can withstand extreme 
temperatures. Often used in IP67-rated devices.
DisAdvantages: Can degrade under UV exposure, although some grades are UV-resistant.
